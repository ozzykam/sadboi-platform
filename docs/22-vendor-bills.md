# Vendor bills

Updated September 21, 2026.

Purchasing → Vendor bills records supplier invoices separately from purchase orders and receiving. A bill can link to a purchase order and stores invoice number/date, due date, subtotal, shipping, tax, currency, status, and notes. Total is calculated as subtotal + shipping + tax.

Bill status is Draft, Received, Approved, Paid, or Void. Saving a bill does not pay the supplier, change inventory, or post a journal entry yet. The first financial slice is a controlled invoice register; payment attempts, allocations, and balanced accounting entries are next.

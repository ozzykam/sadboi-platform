# Refunds and disputes

Updated September 21, 2026.

Payments now support separate refund records linked to settled payments. A refund stores amount, reason, date, external reference, and status: Requested, Approved, Settled, or Rejected. Settled refunds cannot exceed the original settled payment amount.

Refunds preserve the original payment and do not automatically rewrite vendor bills or journal entries. Provider disputes, account reconciliation, and reversal journals remain future work.

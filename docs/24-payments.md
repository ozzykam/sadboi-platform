# Payment attempts and settlements

Updated September 21, 2026.

Purchasing → Payments records attempts against received or approved vendor bills. Each record stores amount, method, date, reference, status, and notes. Settled amounts cannot exceed the bill total; failed and void attempts remain history.

Payment records do not initiate transfers, allocate one payment across multiple bills, or post journal entries. When settled payments exactly cover a bill, the bill is marked Paid in the same transaction; partial, failed, and void attempts leave it open. Use the register to reconcile external bank/card activity until provider integrations and payment allocation workflows are added.
